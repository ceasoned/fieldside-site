Fieldside Track and XC

Timing and results for cross country and track coaches.

Fieldside Track and XC is an iPhone app that times races and practices, keeps every meet result, and turns them into PR boards, rankings, and meet entries.

Race timing

Time cross country races with splits, start on the horn, and combine timing from two phones.

Track & field meets

Enter results by event, including relays and field marks, or import a results file.

PR board and rankings

Season and all-time PRs for every athlete, and rankings by event and gender.

Meet entries

Fill each event from the rankings within the meet's entry limits.

Practice

Time repeats and workouts for the whole group.

Excel and Google Sheets

Export a season workbook, or keep a Google Sheet up to date for your staff.

Your data stays on your phone

Fieldside has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. Your roster and results are stored on your iPhone, and they only go somewhere when you export them or turn on Google Sheets sync. See the Privacy Policy.

How Fieldside uses Google Sheets

Google Sheets sync is optional. When a coach turns it on and signs in with Google, Fieldside creates a spreadsheet in the coach's own Google Drive and keeps it up to date with the team's PR board, rankings, meet results, and practice times, so the coach can share it with their staff, athletes, and parents using Google's own sharing.

  • See and edit only the files Fieldside creates (drive.file): to create and update the coach's team sheet. Fieldside cannot see anything else in the coach's Drive.
  • See, edit, and create Google Sheets (spreadsheets), asked for only when an assistant coach connects to the head coach's sheet: so the assistant's app can read the team from the sheet the head coach shared with them and add their results to its Add Results tab. Fieldside opens only the sheet whose link the coach pastes in.
  • Email address: to show which Google account is connected.

The app talks directly to Google from the coach's iPhone. Fieldside has no servers, and Google data is never sold, shared, or used for ads. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
