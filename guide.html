Fieldside

A stopwatch, clipboard, and results sheet in one phone. Time cross country races and practice workouts, bring in track meet results, and the app keeps every athlete's history, PRs, and rankings for you.

Status: in beta on TestFlight as Fieldside Track and XC. Cross country timing has been used at real races; track and field and Google Sheets are new. This guide describes what works today and what is coming.

Before the season

Set up your team once

Roster

  • Each athlete has a name, gender, level (Varsity, JV, or your own levels), grad year, bib number, team, and optional photo.
  • Filter by level, gender, or team in one tap, here and anywhere you pick athletes: choose "Varsity girls" and then pick from that list. Archive athletes who leave without losing their results.

Import from a spreadsheet

  • Export your roster from Excel, Numbers, or Google Sheets as CSV and open it in the app. Columns are matched for you.
  • A review screen flags problems (a missing name, an unreadable time) before anything is saved. Athletes already on the roster are updated, not duplicated.
  • Include last season's bests as baseline PRs (for example a PR 5K or PR Long Jump column) so records mean something from day one. Track coaches can bring in a whole PR spreadsheet; see Track records.

Seasons, courses, and meets

  • Create seasons such as "Fall 2026 XC" or "Spring 2027 Track" to track season records.
  • Save the cross country courses you race on with their split points, for example Home 5K timed at 1 mi and 2 mi. Each race keeps its own copy, so editing a course later never changes past results.
  • A meet can hold its location (with a Directions button for Maps) and the link to its official results website.
Race day · cross country

Time a meet from one phone

Create a meet, add its races (Varsity Girls, JV Boys…), pick the runners, and start.

Starting the clock

  • Tap START on the gun, or arm the horn or gun start and the clock begins the instant the sound is heard. Sensitivity goes from Low to Maximum for a far-away start.
  • Standing away from the start? Enter your distance and the app corrects for the sound's travel time. At 100 m that is about 0.29 seconds.
  • False start? For the first 90 seconds a red False Start button sits at the top of the screen. One tap puts the clock back to zero, and on a horn start the microphone starts listening again for the restart.
TAP A NAME

Every runner has a big button. Tap it as they pass. The time is taken the moment your finger lands, and the button flashes green with the split and time.

After the first split, buttons are ordered by each runner's time at the previous split, so the leaders are at the top.

MARK

A pack coming through? Hit MARK for each runner and add the names a moment later. Waiting times line up at the top of the screen.

PHOTO

Switch to the camera and tap the shutter. The time is taken at the tap, and the photo is saved with it so you can zoom in and match the runner later.

Splits

  • The split you're timing (1 mi → 2 mi → Finish) shows at the top. Every time is tagged with that split.
  • When every runner still in the race has a time, the screen moves to the next split and offers Undo. You can also jump to any split yourself, or skip one.
  • Runner buttons never move around while you're tapping. Timed runners just dim in place.

Things go wrong. That's fine.

  • Mark a runner DNF if they drop out. Add an unknown runner for someone not on your list and sort them out after.
  • Undo the last time, change who a time belongs to, or move times to a different split, one at a time or in a group.
  • Can't get back to the finish? Stop Recording keeps everything you captured. Nothing is ever guessed or filled in for you.
  • Need a do-over? Restart Race during a race, Reset Race after it, or Reset All Races for a whole meet.
Review, then complete

After the race, the app shows what's missing, for example "7 finishes missing". Fix what you can, then mark the race complete whenever you're ready, even with gaps. The race keeps a small "not fully recorded" note until the gaps are filled.

Race day · two coaches

Two phones, one race

One coach times the start and the splits, an assistant times the finish on their own phone. Afterward the two recordings combine into one complete race.

Before the race: line up the clocks

Set up the race on both phones (same race, same runners), then pick one:

BOTH START

Both coaches start their clocks at the gun, by tap or horn start. The times already match.

Best choice when both can hear the gun.

SYNC TAP

Assistant too far away to hear the gun? Stand together before the race, open the start screen, count "3, 2, 1", and both tap SYNC.

Exact, even though their clock never starts at the gun.

TIME OF DAY

No shared start and no sync? The app lines up the phones' clocks. Usually within a fraction of a second.

Marked "approximate" and left out of PRs unless you allow it.

After the race: share and merge

  1. The assistant opens the race and taps Share Recording, then sends it by AirDrop, Messages, or email.
  2. Tap the file. The app suggests the matching race (same day, same runners). Or, from the race, tap Merge Another Phone's Recording…
  3. Check the preview. Nothing is saved until you tap Merge.
  4. Tap Merge. Places, pace, and PRs update, and merged times carry a small "merged" label.

What the preview shows

  • Runners matched by name, then bib number. Runners not on your phone are added to your roster. Change any match that's wrong.
  • Both phones have a time for the same runner and split? You choose yours or theirs. Yours is kept unless you pick theirs.
  • Times to add by runner, with a warning if any would end up out of order.
Mistake? Undo it.

Undo Merge removes the merged times and puts back any of yours they replaced, until you mark the race complete. Merging the same file twice adds nothing new.

Practice

Time a workout

One Practice card on the Home screen times sprints and repeats for the whole group.

Plan it, or build it as you go

  • Planned workout: add sets, each with a number of reps, a distance (40 m to 1600 m, yards, or custom) or a fly rep, and the rest between reps. Add a longer rest between sets. For example 3 × 200 m, 2:00 rest then 4 × 100 m, 1:00 rest, with 4:00 between sets.
  • Save a workout you'll use again and start it in one tap next time.
  • Ad hoc: pick each rep's distance as you go, change the rest mid-practice, and tap Finish when you're done.
  • Fly reps: set a run-in and a timed distance, for example Fly 30m with a 20m run-in. Fly reps start with a tap, and fly times keep their own records.

During practice

  • The screen shows where you are, such as Set 2 of 3 · Rep 1 of 2, and during the longer rest it previews the next set.
  • A rest countdown runs between reps. When rest is over the phone sends a notification, even if it's in your pocket or locked. Choose sound, vibration, or off under Settings → Rest Alert.
  • A summary shows every rep for every athlete, set by set, with averages and the fastest and slowest rep. Export it as a spreadsheet.

The practice workbook and sheet

  • Sessions → Export → Export Practice Workbook (or the table button on the Practice screen) makes one Excel file of this season's practice.
  • Practice Bests: each athlete's best rep at every distance, with its date. Weekly Mileage: miles each runner ran in timed sessions each week, counting cross country runs and races and every practice rep. Athlete History: one row per athlete per workout, with every rep, the best, and the average.
  • Then a tab for each week, newest first. Each workout is a block with every athlete's reps. A gray time was left out of records, such as a false start, and isn't in the best or average.
  • Practice Google Sheet keeps the same tabs up to date in your Drive, as its own sheet, separate from the meet sheets. Sign in, tap Start My Team's Sheet, and share it with Google's Share button. It's one way: there's no Add Results tab, and other coaches open it in Google Sheets instead of connecting their app.
Practice times never count as PRs

Each athlete's best practice rep is kept separately as their Practice Best, so you can see how practice compares with meets without mixing them up.

Track & Field
Meets

Track meets and results

Open the Track & Field card on the Home screen. Running events, hurdles, steeplechase, relays, jumps, throws, and multi-events are all built in, and you can add your own events.

A meet

  • Create a track meet with its date and location, and mark it Indoor if it is.
  • See the team's results by event (like the meet program) or by athlete, with places, rounds, and relay legs.

Typing in results

  • Pick an event and type each athlete's time or mark. One screen per event, fast to fill in.
  • Times like 12.84 or 2:05.31; field marks in feet and inches like 18-04.5; or metric like 5.60m. Type DNF, DQ, NH, or FOUL for results without a mark.
  • Each result can carry FAT or Hand, wind, place, and round (prelim, semi, final).
  • Relays have four legs, each with its runner and an optional split.
  • Typed results are preliminary and get a * in the Google Sheet. Turn on From Official Results when you're copying them from the meet's posted results.
Results from the meet

Bring results in instead of typing them

PDF

Open or share a meet's results PDF (the common Hy-Tek printout) or a MileSplit team results printout. The app reads it on the phone and finds your school's athletes.

List every way your school is printed, such as the full name and a short code like "PJPI" for relays.

PASTE

Copy the results text from a web page or email and paste it in. Same reading and matching as a PDF.

CSV

A results spreadsheet with columns like Event, Name, Mark, Place, and Wind. Columns are matched for you.

  • Every import shows a preview first: who matched the roster, what couldn't be read, and other schools' rows skipped.
  • Importing the same results again updates them instead of adding them twice.
  • Results PDFs often list only the school for a relay. Relays brought in without runners are listed so you can add the legs.
  • Heat sheets are programs, not results, so they aren't imported.
Track & Field
Records

PRs, the PR Board, and rankings

How track PRs work

  • Every athlete has a Season PR and an All-time PR in every event. Lower is better for times; higher is better for jumps, throws, and points.
  • FAT and hand times are labeled, never converted. A hand time shows as 11.4h.
  • Wind-aided marks (over +2.0 m/s, or marked "w") don't count as PRs. Indoor and outdoor marks share one PR list, and indoor marks show an i.
  • Leave a result out of PRs if the timing was off. Practice reps count only as Practice Best.
  • Each athlete's profile groups records into Track, Field, Relays, Cross Country, and Practice, with a progress chart for every event. Team Bests lists the best on the team in each event.

PR Board

Your season spreadsheet, kept for you: every athlete down the side, every event across. Switch between Season PR, All-time PR, and Practice Best, and filter by level, gender, or team.

Bring in your season spreadsheet

  • Track & Field → Import Season Spreadsheet reads the PR spreadsheet you already keep: an Excel (.xlsx) or CSV file with one row per athlete and one column per event.
  • It finds name, gender, and grade columns and event columns like 100, 1600, 100/110H, LJ, SP, or PENT. X and ALT in relay columns become relay pools.
  • Marks come in as baseline Season PRs, so meet results beat them as the season goes on. Athletes not on the roster can be added. Importing again updates instead of duplicating.
Track & Field
Before a meet

Stack a meet

Open a meet and tap Entries to decide who runs what.

  • Set the limits: entries per event (3 by default) and events per athlete (4 by default).
  • Mark athletes who can't compete, with a reason: Injured, Sick, Other sport, Cut, or Absent. They're taken out of the stack.
  • Suggest Entries fills every event from the rankings. The best athletes are placed first across all events, so a top athlete gets their strongest events. Relays take their X pool, with ALT runners as alternates, which don't count toward an athlete's limit.
  • Change anything by hand. Each event lists everyone ranked by seed, with athletes who have no mark yet at the bottom. Hand entries are kept when you suggest again.
  • Export the entries with seeds, in meet-program order, for the meet director or the bus list.
Track & Field
Sharing

Excel and Google Sheets

Excel files

  • Export Season Workbook on the Track & Field screen makes one Excel file with the PR Board, a Relays sheet, Girls and Boys Rankings, and a sheet for each meet this season.
  • The PR Board sheet is for individual events. The Relays sheet lists every relay this season by event, fastest first, with its time, meet, place, and the four runners in leg order (with splits when you have them). SB marks the season best.
  • The PR Board, Rankings, and each meet also export on their own, as Excel or CSV. Meet sheets mark PR (lifetime best) and SB (season best).
  • The PR Board sheet uses the same name and grade columns as your season spreadsheet, so you can edit PRs and bring them back in with Import Season Spreadsheet. Relay pools (X and ALT) are set on the app's PR Board.

Google Sheets

  • Track & Field → Google Sheets → Sign In with Google → Start My Team's Sheet. Fieldside creates a sheet in your Google Drive and keeps it up to date: when you tap Sync Now, and automatically when you leave the app if anything changed and the phone is online.
  • Fieldside can only see files it creates, not the rest of your Drive.
  • Share the sheet with athletes, parents, and assistants using Google's own Share button. It holds athletes' names and results, so share it only with people who should see them.
  • The sheet's tabs: How to Use, Add Results, PR Board, Relays, Girls Rankings, Boys Rankings, and one tab per meet.
  • The app is the source: every tab except Add Results is rewritten on each sync, so changes typed there are replaced. Start a New Sheet each season to keep last season's sheet as it was.

How it flows

  1. An assistant adds a row: date, meet, event, athlete (or Girls A / Boys A for a relay), and the time or mark.
  2. On the next sync Fieldside checks every new row and writes a Status: "Waiting for coach review", or ⚠ and what to fix, such as a name not on the roster or a mark it can't read. Whoever typed it fixes the row, and it's checked again.
  3. You open Google Sheets → Review Results from the Sheet. The Track & Field screen shows how many are waiting. Rows are grouped by meet; a meet the app doesn't have yet is marked New meet and created when you import.
  4. Tap a row to leave it out, then Import. Imported rows are marked Imported ✓ in the sheet and never come in twice.

Corrections

  • To fix a mark, add a new row for the same athlete, event, meet, and round. The review shows Replaces 12.34 so you know it changes a result already in the app.
  • If someone edits a row while you're reviewing it, that row is skipped and shows up again next time, so you always import what you saw.
  • Results typed into Add Results are official. To clear a *, add the result from the meet's official results, even if it's the same mark.

Setting it up

1 · Head coach
  1. Track & Field → Google Sheets → Sign In with Google → Start My Team's Sheet.
  2. Open the sheet, tap Share, and add each coach's Google account as an Editor.
  3. In Fieldside tap Copy Sheet Link and text it to the coaches.
2 · Other coach
  1. Track & Field → Google Sheets → Sign In with Google with the account the sheet was shared with.
  2. Paste the link under Connect to Head Coach's Sheet.
  3. Google asks once to let Fieldside open sheets shared with you. Allow it.
3 · Every sync
  1. Load: the head coach's roster, meets, results, relays, and PRs come in.
  2. Send: your new track results go to Add Results, marked with your name.
  3. The head coach reviews and imports them; on your next sync they're the head coach's.

Good to know

  • The head coach's version wins. Loading updates what the head coach changed and adds what's new. It never deletes anything in the other coach's app.
  • Athletes and meets match by name, so a coach who already had the roster won't get doubles. Their own results that the head coach already has aren't sent back.
  • Changing a result you already sent sends it again; the head coach sees Replaces … when reviewing.
  • Sync runs on Sync Now, and automatically when you leave the app if the phone is online.
  • Practice and cross country stay on each coach's own phone; only track and field results are shared this way.
  • Only the head coach's account needs the sheet in their Drive. Every coach who signs in to Fieldside with Google must be on the app's tester list during the beta.
After · cross country

Results and records, kept for you

Race results

  • Places, every split, the time between splits, finish, and pace per mile or kilometer. Missing times show as "—".
  • Times timed or typed in the app carry a * as preliminary. Official times have no star: imported from the meet's results file, typed into the sheet's Add Results tab, or typed in the app with From Official Results on.
  • Type in a missing time, or import the official results file from the meet. Runners are matched by bib and name, and added times are labeled so you know where they came from.
  • Turn an unknown runner into a roster athlete, or link them to someone you already have.

Your team

  • Each race shows your scoring five and your 6th and 7th runners, with how far each is behind the 5th.
  • Top-5 average and 1–5 spread (the gap from your first to your fifth runner), plus the spread at every split so you can see where the pack broke up.
  • Team score from your top five's overall places. Type the places in or import them with the official results.
  • A season trend compares every race with the same name, such as all your Varsity Girls races.

PRs and history

  • Season records (SR) and personal records (PR) update themselves. A new best gets a badge in the results.
  • Each athlete has a history for every event with a progress chart. Leave a time out of PRs if the course was short or the timing was off.
  • Team Bests ranks your athletes in any event, for a season or all time, by gender, level, or team.

Export

  • Send any race, meet, athlete, or season to a spreadsheet (CSV) by email, AirDrop, or Files. Opens in Excel, Numbers, or Google Sheets.
  • Export Season Workbook on the Cross Country screen makes one Excel file for the season. See the next page.

Why you can trust the times

Built for the start line

Works without signalTiming, imports, and merging two phones by AirDrop all work offline. Only Google Sheets needs a connection, and it catches up when you're back online.
Survives a crashIf the app closes mid-race, it reopens with the clock still running and every time kept.
Fingertip accurateTimes are taken when your finger touches the screen, not when it lifts.
Track roundingTimes are shown to hundredths and round up, so a hand time never looks faster than it was.
Screen stays onThe phone doesn't go to sleep while you're timing.
Your data stays yoursResults live on your phone and in its iCloud backup. The Google Sheet is in your own Drive, and Fieldside can't see your other files.
