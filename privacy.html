FFieldside Track and XC

Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

Fieldside Track and XC ("Fieldside") is an iPhone app for cross country and track and field coaches. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. The short version: your team's data stays on your iPhone. Fieldside has no accounts, no servers, no ads, and no analytics. The developer never receives your team's data.

Information you enter

Coaches use Fieldside to record information about their athletes and meets, such as:

  • Athlete names, gender, graduation year or grade, bib numbers, team and level, and notes
  • Times, marks, splits, places, meet entries, relay teams, and personal records
  • Meets, courses, seasons, and practice workouts
  • Photos you choose to add, such as an athlete photo or a photo taken at a finish line

This information is stored only on your iPhone, inside the app. It is not sent to the developer or to any other company unless you choose to share it as described below. Deleting the app, or using Settings → Delete All Data in the app, removes it from your phone.

Fieldside is a tool for coaches. Athletes do not use the app or create accounts, and the app does not collect information directly from students.

Microphone

If you use Sound Start, the app listens through the microphone for a starting horn, gun, or clap so the timer can start on its own. The sound is analyzed on the phone while the timer is waiting for a start. It is not recorded, saved, or sent anywhere.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you take a photo in the app, for example of runners crossing a split or finish line as proof of a time. If you pick an athlete photo from your photo library, only that photo is used. Photos are stored in the app on your phone.

Sharing you choose to do

Information leaves your phone only when you send it:

  • Exports. Excel, CSV, and other files you export go wherever you send them with the iOS share sheet (for example email, Files, or AirDrop).
  • Merging recordings. When two coaches combine timing from two phones, the race recording is sent directly between the phones with AirDrop or the share sheet.
  • Google Sheets (optional). Described in the next section.

Google Sheets sync (optional)

If you turn on Google Sheets in the app, you sign in with your Google account and Fieldside keeps a spreadsheet up to date in your own Google Drive. Here is exactly what that involves:

  • What Fieldside can access. Fieldside asks for permission to create and edit the spreadsheets it makes (drive.file) and to see your email address, so the app can show which Google account is connected. If you join another coach's sheet, Fieldside also asks for permission to read and edit Google Sheets (spreadsheets), which it uses only to open the sheet whose link you paste in. Fieldside does not read your other Drive files, your email, or your contacts.
  • What is written to the sheet. Your PR Board, rankings, meet results, relay results, and the information needed for other coaches on your staff to load the same team (athletes, seasons, meets, and results). Rows that you or your assistant coaches type into the sheet's Add Results tab are read back into the app only after you review them.
  • Where it goes. The app talks directly to Google from your phone. Your data goes to your Google account and to the people you share the sheet with, and nowhere else. The developer has no access to it.
  • Sign-in. Your Google sign-in is kept in the iPhone Keychain on your phone. Disconnecting in the app signs out and revokes Fieldside's access. You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting does not delete the spreadsheet; it stays in your Drive until you delete it.

Fieldside's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the Sheets sync you turned on. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not transferred to anyone else, and not read by people.

Your use of Google services is also covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

What Fieldside does not do

  • No advertising and no tracking across apps or websites
  • No analytics or usage tracking inside the app
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone
  • No Fieldside accounts or servers

Crash reports

If you have turned on sharing with app developers in your iPhone's settings, or you are a TestFlight tester, Apple may send the developer anonymous crash reports. These contain technical details about the crash, not your team's data. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Schools and student records

Coaches are responsible for following their school's or district's rules about student information, including what they put in a shared Google Sheet and whom they share it with. Because the data stays on the coach's phone and in the coach's own Google account, the coach and the school stay in control of it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
